Welcome to Digital Heart Twin

This app records your heartbeat using your phone's motion sensors and builds a personal digital heart twin — a model that learns what YOUR normal heart looks like so it can detect changes over time.

The Guide tab will walk you through the full setup step by step. It takes about 5–10 minutes across several sessions.
